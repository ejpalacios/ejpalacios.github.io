A Practical Guide to Trading and Tracing for the Energy Blockchain

## Abstract
This chapter presents the outcomes of the experimental implementation of the architecture described in Chap.{~}4running a hardware testbed for the emulation of domestic electrical load profiles. Besides it shows how this experimental scenario integrates both with the legacy metering architecture and with the blockchain platform. Finally, the integrated use with an Energy Management System designed on purpose is also described with the aim of ensuring intervention and automating the end user&apos;s response to a DR event.
