I am a Research Fellow at the [Sustainability Institute](https://www.ucc.ie/en/sustainability-institute/) of University College Cork (UCC) and [MaREI](https://www.marei.ie), the Research Ireland Centre for Energy, Climate, and Marine Research and Innovation.

Previously, I was a Research Associate and Marie Skłodowska-Curie SoE fellow at [KU Leuven](https://www.esat.kuleuven.be/electa) and the research centre [EnergyVille](https://energyville.be/en/), Belgium (2020-2024), and a Postdoctoral Researcher at [Aalborg University](https://www.energy.aau.dk/research/research-groups/crom), Denmark (2018-2020).

I hold a PhD in Advanced Computation and Energy (2018), an MSc in Distributed Renewable Energy (2013), and a BE in Electronic Engineering (2012) from the University of Córdoba, Spain.

My research areas include data-driven modelling and forecasting of energy flexibility, advanced metering infrastructures for user-centric demand response, privacy-friendly data processing, IoT architectures and protocols for energy services, and stochastic modelling of residential energy demand.

I have co-authored {{ site.publications | size }} [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) in peer-reviewed international journals, conferences, and books. I am also a Senior Member of the Institute of Electrical and Electronic Engineers (IEEE).
